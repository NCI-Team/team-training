# Goals

- The trainee will understand what Multicast is.
- The trainee will learn the differences between multicast and other routing methods.
- The trainee will understand when and how multicast is used.

## Tasks

- What is Multicast and how does it fundamentally differ from Unicast, Broadcast, and Anycast?
- How are Multicast IP addresses structured, and how do they differ from standard interface IP assignments?
- What is IGMP and how does it bridge the gap between a host and a router?
- Why is IGMP Snooping a critical feature on Layer 2 switches?
- What is PIM Dense Mode, and what is the primary problem with it?
- What is a Rendezvous Point (RP) in multicast routing, and how do routers find it?
- What is PIM Sparse Mode and Sparse-Dense Mode?
